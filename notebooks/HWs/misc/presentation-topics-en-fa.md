# Student Presentation Topics (English & Persian)

A list of **20 fresh** presentation topics suitable for a one‑hour talk at the end of the machine‑learning course.  
Each topic is **not covered in the existing lecture notebooks**, but is related to the course themes (supervised/unsupervised learning, deep learning, probabilistic models, time‑series, etc.) and can be complemented by a recent research article or a short demo.

The format is **English – Persian** for every entry.  
Students may choose one topic, prepare ~10 slides, and present for ~60 minutes (45 min talk + 15 min discussion).

---

**English**: Techniques (few‑shot, chain‑of‑thought, instruction tuning) and evaluation metrics for LLMs like GPT‑4, Claude.

**English**: How adding noise to gradients or model updates guarantees privacy; case studies on DP‑SGD for deep nets.

## 12. Self‑Supervised Speech Representation (Wav2Vec 2.0)
**English**: How wav2vec 2.0 learns from raw audio; downstream tasks (ASR, emotion detection).  
**Persian**: نحوه یادگیری نمایش‌های خودمنده از صدا خام توسط wav2vec 2.0; کارهای بعدی (ASR، تشخیص احساس).

**English**: Model quantization, pruning, and deploying a tiny CNN or decision tree on Arduino / ESP‑32 for activity recognition.  
**Persian**: کمی supplied quantization, pruning, و رویایی یک CNN یا درخت کوچک روی Arduino / ESP‑32 برای تشخیص فعالیت.

## 14. Counterfactual Reasoning in Reinforcement Learning
**English**: Generating “what‑if” state‑action alternatives and their impact on policy improvement.

**English**: TP/FP parity, equalized odds, and a step‑by‑step audit checklist for a real dataset.

## 16. Active Learning for Label‑Efficient Training
**English**: Principles of active learning, query strategies (uncertainty-based, diversity-based), real‑world applications in medical imaging and text classification.
**Persian**: اصول یادگیری فعال، استراتجیک سوال کردن (ناويینی، تنوع)، کاربردهای واقعی در پزشکی و klasyfikation متن.

**English**: SimCLR, MoCo, BYOL; how contrastive losses learn visual representations; downstream tasks (classification, detection).
**Persian**: SimCLR, MoCo, BYOL؛ نحوه یادگیری نمایش Contrasting از طریق losses Comparing؛Funcs tasks عقب (classification, detection).

## 18. Online Learning and Concept Drift
**English**: Algorithms for data streams, regret bounds, handling changing environments; applications to network security.
**Persian**: الگوریتم‌ها برای پی‌درایوهای داده، regret bounds، مدیریت محیط‌های تغییرکننده؛ کاربردهای امنیتی شبکه.

## 19. Gaussian Process Regression for Uncertainty Quantification
**English**: Non‑parametric Bayesian model, predictive mean and variance, applications in robotics and hyperparameter optimization.
**Persian**: مدل بی‑پارامتریک Bayesi، معیار پیش‌بینی و 분散، کاربردهای رباتیک و بهینه‌سازی hyperparameter.

## 20. VC Dimension and Model Complexity
**English**: Explanation of VC (Vapnik-Chervonenkis) dimension, its role in measuring model capacity, uniform convergence, and sample complexity bounds. Connection to generalization error and PAC learning.

### How to Use This List
1. **Pick a topic** that resonates with your interests or final project. 
2. **Find 2‑3 recent papers** (e.g., from NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, or arXiv published in the last 2 years).  
3. **Create ~10 slides**: motivation, key algorithm, experimental results (one slide per paper), take‑aways, and future directions.  
4. **Practice a 45‑minute talk** (≈1 slide/minute) and leave 15 minutes for Q&A.  
5. **Submit a one‑page summary** (bullet points) to the instructor after the presentation.
